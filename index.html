How marking works

Correct 100%   Close 70%   Not very close 35%   Wrong 0%

Numeric answers are marked by percent error against the worked solution (within tolerance, 3×, 10×). You may type units (kN, MPa, mm…) and scientific notation (3.2e6 or 3.2x10^6). A right magnitude with a wrong sign or power of ten earns partial credit. Written answers are marked against a weighted rubric of key points. A hint costs 25% of the mark. Written answers and quick calculations are timed, with more time for harder questions; longer calculations are untimed. Each percent is one unit of altitude.
